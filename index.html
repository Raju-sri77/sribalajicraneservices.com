Meet the Founder

Building Trust,
One Lift at a Time

"Every project we take on carries someone's dream — a building, a bridge, an industry. Our job is to make sure we lift that dream safely, on time, and within budget."

With over two decades of hands-on experience in the crane and heavy lifting industry, Sri founded Titan Crane Services with a simple mission: bring reliability and professionalism to every job site. Today, Titan operates a fleet of 50+ modern XCMG cranes and serves leading construction companies across the region.
